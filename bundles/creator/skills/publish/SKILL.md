# Publish

Use this when a video has a script in `script/outline.md` and you are preparing the text that goes with the upload. Write the result to `publish.md` in the project root. Leave a section empty rather than inventing a claim the script does not support.

This skill follows the same idea as two public projects, and it does not copy their text: [calesthio/OpenMontage](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage), which treats a script and a shot plan as the start of a production, and [danielrosehill/Claude-Video-Editor-Plugin](https://github.com/danielrosehill/Claude-Video-Editor-Plugin), which turns editing work into a written handoff. Stay inside this project's files.

## Fill publish.md

- Title: one line a person would understand without the video. Use the script's subject, not a tease that hides it.
- Description: two short paragraphs. The first says what the video shows. The second says who it is for and what they can do afterwards.
- Chapters: a timestamp and a name for each section in the outline. Timestamps come from the edit or from the shot list's durations. If you have not watched the cut, leave the times as `00:00:00` and say they are untimed.
- Tags: a handful of words from the script. No tag that the video does not cover.
- Thumbnail brief: what is on the thumbnail, the few words of text, and what must stay readable at a small size. This is a brief, not an image file.

Do not upload the video, create a channel, or sign into YouTube or any other account as part of this skill.
